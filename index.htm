Metro Restaurant & Bar — Itahari, Koshi Province
Our Origin

Born from Itahari's
Rising Ambition

In 2014, when Itahari was still finding its identity as eastern Nepal's fastest-growing commercial hub, Metro opened its doors with a conviction that this city deserved more than ordinary. We saw a community hungry for experiences that matched their aspirations — people who worked hard, travelled widely, and came home wanting a place that felt genuinely premium.

We built Metro not as a restaurant, but as a destination. A space where the aromas of slow-cooked Nepali curries and tandoor smoke mingled with the cool hum of carefully chosen music, where the warm glow of amber lighting turned strangers into friends and ordinary Tuesday evenings into something worth remembering.

Culinary Philosophy

"Food is memory. Every dish we serve carries the weight of tradition and the lightness of joy."

Our kitchen is guided by a singular principle — use the finest local produce, honour classical techniques, and plate with intention. Our chefs draw from the diverse food cultures converging in this region: Nepali hill traditions, Madheshi warmth, Tibetan influence, and the vibrant theatre of Indian cuisine. The result is a menu that is deeply familiar yet always surprising.

Our Journey

A Decade of Excellence

2014
Grand Opening
Metro Restaurant and Bar opens at Mahendra Highway Chowk with a 60-seat restaurant and intimate bar. The response from Itahari's community is immediate and overwhelming.
2016
Saturday Night Live Launch
We introduce weekly DJ nights, transforming Metro into the first dedicated nightlife venue in Sunsari District. The format quickly becomes Itahari's most anticipated weekly event.
2018
Kitchen Expansion & Tandoor
A full kitchen renovation introduces our clay tandoor and live grill station, expanding the menu to include our now-legendary tandoor platters and wood-fired specialties.
2020
Bar Programme Relaunch
We hire our first dedicated head bartender and launch the Metro cocktail programme, including our signature Himalayan Gin and seasonal Terai-inspired drinks.
2024
Decade Celebration
Metro celebrates 10 years with a sold-out gala dinner, a limited anniversary menu, and a renewed commitment to being the heartbeat of Itahari's dining and nightlife scene.
The Space

Designed to Enchant

Our interiors were conceived as a conversation between old Nepal and modern aspiration. Dark teak accents, hand-hammered copper bar fixtures, and hand-painted wall motifs inspired by Madhesh folk art create an atmosphere that is unmistakably Nepali — and utterly contemporary.

The restaurant floor seats 80 guests across intimate two-tops and generous banquette booths. The bar is a 14-seat centrepiece of polished brass and dark marble. A private dining room accommodates 20 guests for celebrations, corporate dinners, and family occasions.
